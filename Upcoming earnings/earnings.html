What changed in the actual business?

Compare revenue, expenses, profit or loss, and cash flow across periods. Then read management's explanation and risks. A strong brand story, including one connected to Black culture, is not a substitute for financial information.

Reveal a learning tip

Compare the price with the previous close: a higher price means a gain, and a lower price means a decline. A daily price change is not an investment recommendation. Consider the business or fund, its risks, fees, and your time horizon.
