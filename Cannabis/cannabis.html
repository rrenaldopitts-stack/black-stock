Culture-to-capital challenge

What would accountability look like?

Look for ownership and leadership disclosures, financing terms, regulatory risks, and evidence behind community-impact claims. A mission statement alone does not verify ownership or outcomes.

Reflection for your knowledge circle

How would I check a community-benefit claim?

Reflect privately or talk with someone you trust. No answer is collected, and you do not need to share financial details.

Cannabis businesses can face changing laws, licensing restrictions, financing challenges, and substantial losses. Values alignment does not remove investment risk.
