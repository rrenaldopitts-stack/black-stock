Culture-to-capital challenge

What does the token actually give me?

Read the token's rights and restrictions. A token does not automatically give you company equity, voting power, or a claim on revenue. Check who controls the project and how tokens are distributed.

Reflection for your knowledge circle

Would I understand this project without its community slogan?

Reflect privately or talk with someone you trust. No answer is collected, and you do not need to share financial details.

Crypto is not stock. Prices can collapse, access can fail, and legal protections vary. Community identity is not evidence that a project is safe.
