Culture-to-capital challenge

Whose story is being told?

Pick a familiar brand. Ask who owns it, how it earns money, and whether the public shares represent the business you have in mind. Visibility in Black culture does not establish Black ownership.

Reflection for your knowledge circle

What would I research if I had never seen this brand online?

Reflect privately or talk with someone you trust. No answer is collected, and you do not need to share financial details.

A widely discussed stock can still be expensive, concentrated, or financially weak. Compare business results and risks rather than counting mentions.
