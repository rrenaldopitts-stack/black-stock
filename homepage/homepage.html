Why TradeX?

Financial education should feel accessible and relevant. TradeX centers Black learners and the goals, families, and futures they want to invest in. Everyone is welcome, and no prior investing experience is expected.

What works in this HTML/CSS version?

Use the journey library and shared navigation to explore lessons, answer knowledge checks, reveal explanations, and complete a checklist. The journey layout uses connected learning stops, chapter headers, a native More menu, and bottom navigation on desktop and mobile. Category pages separate lessons, knowledge checks, and practice using ordinary links and CSS. It uses HTML and CSS only; journey maps do not track completion. Every page links to investing lessons, Black business learning, and culture. Culture, Black business examples, and community prompts now live on the Culture page. The fictional portfolio, chart, and holdings live in the Numbers lab. Those examples are imagined learning situations, not a verified company directory. There are no accounts, saved balances, live prices, trades, or persistent rewards.
