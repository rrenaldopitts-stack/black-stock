Culture-to-capital challenge

Who controls access to the asset?

Learn the difference between platform custody and self-custody, including withdrawal rules and the risk of losing access. Never share seed phrases or private keys, including with someone presenting themselves as a community mentor.

Reflection for your knowledge circle

What would I need to understand before trusting a platform?

Reflect privately or talk with someone you trust. No answer is collected, and you do not need to share financial details.

Crypto is not stock. Platforms can fail, withdrawals can be restricted, and assets can lose all value. Trading availability is not a recommendation.
