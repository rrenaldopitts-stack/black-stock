Explain investing / What does a share actually give me?

A share is a small ownership interest in a company, not a promise of profit. Prices can fall, dividends are not guaranteed, and buying platform stock does not give you an artist's royalties. Culture can spark curiosity; research helps you understand what you own.

Research Black business / What should I verify first?

Check what the business sells, who owns it, and whether it is public or private. Black leadership, Black ownership, and cultural relevance are different claims. Verify current ownership and voting control with reliable sources; do not assume a familiar brand has public shares.
